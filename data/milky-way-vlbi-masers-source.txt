The catalog contains 199 VLBI trigonometric-parallax measurements of molecular masers associated with young high-mass star-forming regions. The measurements come primarily from the BeSSeL Survey and the Japanese VERA project, with additional EVN and Australian LBA results.

Coordinate transform:

- heliocentric distance = 1 / parallax in kpc, for parallax in milliarcseconds
- ICRS coordinates are transformed with the standard J2000 ICRS-to-Galactic rotation matrix
- x = distance * cos(b) * sin(l)
- y = 8.3 kpc - distance * cos(b) * cos(l)
- z = distance * sin(b)
- Galactic Center = (0, 0, 0)
- Sun = (0, 8.3, 0) kpc, matching the shared display frame

The renderer uses open circles so these high-precision parallax anchors remain distinct from the Hou & Han compilation. Some Reid sources also appear in that earlier literature compilation; the layer is shown as an updated measurement catalog, not counted as 199 wholly independent objects.

Scientific limitations:

- Inverse parallax is used directly and its uncertainty becomes asymmetric in distance, especially for the most distant sources.
- The catalog traces young high-mass star-forming regions, not the full stellar population.
- Selection reflects the sky coverage and targets of the contributing VLBI programs.
