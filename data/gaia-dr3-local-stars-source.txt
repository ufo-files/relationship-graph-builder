Local detail sample (gaia-dr3-3d-local-stars.csv):
SELECT TOP 30000 source_id,ra,dec,parallax,phot_g_mean_mag
FROM gaiadr3.gaia_source
WHERE parallax_over_error > 10
  AND parallax > 2
  AND random_index < 1000000000
ORDER BY random_index

Rendering:
- Distance in parsecs is the direct inverse of positive parallax in arcseconds.
- ICRS right ascension and declination are transformed with the standard ICRS-to-Galactic rotation matrix.
- The default corpus-neighborhood view displays the 11,639 rows at or within 1,000 light-years, covering every reviewed fixed-position corpus target; the full 30,000-row sample remains checked in.
- No arm assignment, density interpolation, extinction correction, or synthetic star is added.

Important limitation:
Inverse parallax becomes increasingly uncertain with distance. This deterministic local sample is an observational point cloud, not a complete or volume-corrected census.
