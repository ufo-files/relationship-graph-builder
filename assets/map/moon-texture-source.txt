Rendered asset: moon-paper.png (1024 × 512 PNG)
Source asset: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg
Source page: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
Credit: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio

NASA's CGI Moon Kit color map is assembled from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter
Camera Wide Angle Camera data. The checked-in JPEG is the unmodified 2K asset
published by NASA. The rendered PNG is a resized grayscale edge-map derivative
used to match the globe's flat paper-and-ink visual style.

ImageMagick generation command:
magick moon-lroc-color.jpg -resize 1024x512! -colorspace Gray -gaussian-blur 0x1 -edge 1 -normalize -threshold 28% -negate +level-colors '#777777','#ecebe4' -colorspace sRGB moon-paper.png

Physical values used by the map renderer:
- Moon equatorial radius: 1,737.4 km
- Earth equatorial radius: 6,371.0 km
- Moon orbital period and synchronous rotation period: 27.322 Earth days
- Moon orbit inclination: 5.145 degrees

The interactive map visually compresses the orbital period to 30 seconds and the
displayed Earth–Moon gap so the motion and map remain usable. These display values
are not presented as physical scale.

Physical data reference: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/by-the-numbers/
