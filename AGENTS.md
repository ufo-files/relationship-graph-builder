1. When opening a PR, always include a local Tailscale address where a human reviewer on the Tailnet can test. Example: http://100.87.114.77:4173
2. Always include a screenshot of the before and after shots of the screen(s) being worked on.
3. Check for comments on the PR from Codex or `ufo-files` and address them, comment on the thread, and resolve the conversation.
4. If the comments changed the screens in the PR, generate a new after shot.
5. Delete your branch when the PR is merged.
