# Book title integrity audit — September 23, 2026

The published catalog contained transcript fragments masquerading as books. Examples include **I'm**, **I've**, **I'll**, **American**, **Nick**, **Joe**, and **UFOs**. Capitalization-based extraction after the word “book” accepted conversational clauses and truncated mixed-case titles. Numeric strings and dangling title connectors also passed.

## Correction

Unreviewed titles now need quotation or an explicit title assertion and a clear boundary. Bare words after “in the book” no longer establish an unknown book. Standalone contractions, numeric strings, and dangling connectors are rejected. Sentence predicates are separated from titles, while titles such as *Mr. Kant is Dead* remain intact. Paired quotation marks preserve internal apostrophes. Reviewed titles still recognize lowercase transcription variants.

Added *American Cosmic* and Diana Walsh Pasulka to the reviewed registry, supported by the [author's bibliography](https://dwpasulka.com/books/). **American** is not an alias: the archive also uses that fragment for other titles, so merging every occurrence would misattribute evidence.

## Archived-source rebuild

Ran the baseline and corrected builders with `--require-data`, English paired data, and empty claims against the same selected source files from machine-data revision `e5a08eda92831eab9b9dc24046ead81e1b729f7f`. Both builds accepted 34 documents. Selection used stored evidence for reported fragments and legitimate-title controls.

The book list changed from 21 entities to 9. The rebuilt output excludes **I'm**, **I've**, **I'll**, **American**, **UFO**, **UFOs**, **Nick**, **Joe**, **Kate McCann**, **On Growth**, and **1967**. It corrects **The UFO Evidence is** to **The UFO Evidence** and includes **American Cosmic**. Controls **Communion**, **Majestic**, and **Home** remain. Unverified fragments **Mothman** and **Thunderbirds** are also withheld; their removal is not a claim that no such books exist.

This is a focused source rebuild, not a completed full-corpus rebuild. Generated production catalogs are regenerated by the publishing workflow, not edited by hand.
